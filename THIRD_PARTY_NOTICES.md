## OpenStreetMap data and tiles

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the ODbL.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

The historical viewer requests the standard raster map from https://tile.openstreetmap.org/ and shows attribution on the map. Requests identify this extension using X-Requested-With, as documented at https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Referer for contexts without an HTTP Referer. It does not prefetch tiles, provide offline downloads, spoof Referer/User-Agent, or override the browser's cache behavior.
